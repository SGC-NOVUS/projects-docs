## 2. Description of the Service & Non-Custodial Nature

2.1. Novus Life provides analytical, mathematical, and organizational tools for personal and family tracking:
- **Property & Resource Accounting:** Calculating consumption of utilities (electricity, water, natural gas, thermal energy, HOA fees) across 24 configurable tariff patterns.
- **Wealth & Cash Flow Management:** Tracking bank cards, loans, multi-currency accounts, and debt repayment timelines.
- **Adaptive Budgeting:** Implementing the 4 Envelopes methodology, two-tier category limits, and targeted savings goals.
- **Work Schedules & Payroll:** Recording shift rosters, night premiums, overtime, and estimated net salaries.

2.2. **Non-Custodial Architecture:**  
Novus Life is strictly a record-keeping and calculation application. The Service **never** executes financial transactions on your behalf, does not hold or custody monetary funds, does not initiate bank transfers, and does not hold custody of payment instruments.

2.3. **Local-First Data Model:**  
All primary records are stored locally on your device within browser-managed IndexedDB. Synchronization with self-hosted PostgreSQL or Supabase servers is initiated at your sole discretion.
